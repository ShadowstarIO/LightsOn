# Outdoors

Separate from venues. A pocket of street around you, not a house and not a whole zone.

**Audit** and stay near where you started for about a minute. Walking off pauses or cancels it. Same area waits before another audit.

Optional one-word scene (RP, Party, Hunt, and so on). If it looks like friends or Free Company, LightsOn asks whether it is a private gathering.

Reports last about four hours. Names are never sent.
