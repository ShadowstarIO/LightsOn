# What the lantern reads

Copy is meant to sit between OOC and IC. A roleplayer can read it as news. Anyone else can still tell what the game is doing.

Nothing here is uploaded as names or a headcount. The server only ever gets **lanterns lit** or **wrapped up early**.

## States

| In LightsOn | In the game | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lanterns lit** / happening | — | Enough signs of company on the listed plot, last ~20 minutes |
| **Wrapped up early** | — | Listed hours say open, but the door and yard look quiet |
| **Marked open** | venue schedule | FFXIV Venues says these hours are on. Not occupancy. |
| **Opens …** | venue schedule | Next listed hours. An invitation, not a report. |

## Company (this build)

A **patron** is another person on the plot. Not you. Friends and Free Company can be left out in Settings.

**Enough company** = 3 patrons after those filters. That is the only score in this build.

| In LightsOn | In the game | Points (later) | Now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Patron** | other PC on the plot | 1 each, cap 3 | scored |
| **In character** | Role-Playing status | 1 | listed, not scored yet |
| **Seeking company** | Looking for Party | 1 | listed, not scored yet |
| **A gathering is posted** | Party Finder | 1 | listed, not scored yet |
| **At the bench** | Melding Materia | 1 | listed, not scored yet |
| **Someone reached out** | /tell from someone on this plot | 2 | listed, not scored yet |
| **An exchange** | trade on this plot | 2 | listed, not scored yet |
| **Voices nearby** | say from this plot | 1 | off unless you turn it on; not scored yet |

Quiet / wrapped up early = not enough company, and the door/yard check.

## Filters

- **Leave friends out** — people on your Friend List are not patrons.
- **Leave Free Company out** — matching company tag is not a patron.

Those lists never leave your machine.
