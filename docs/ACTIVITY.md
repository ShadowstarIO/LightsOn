# What the lantern reads

| In LightsOn | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Lanterns Lit** | Enough company on that layer |
| **Quiet Halls / Yard Quiet** | Hours said open; that layer looks empty |
| **open?** | Hours on, no occupancy yet |
| **open!** | Lean toward company |
| **open~** | Split |
| **"open"** | Hours on, audit quiet |

Yard and inside list separately. The room weighs more. You have to be on that property. Houses are ward and plot. Apartments are ward and apartment.

Company is counted on your machine: people nearby, plus optional IC, glances, emotes, and chat *happening* — never the text. Friends and Free Company can be left out. Enough company is a local score; names and the score never leave.

Older reports fade so a fresh quiet can beat a stale busy. Hours off: reports drop.

The log book is a pair from two lists (*Kind Crowd*, *Warm Food*). Flavor only, not an occupancy point.
